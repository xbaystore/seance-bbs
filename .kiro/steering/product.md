# Product — Séance BBS

## Vision

Séance BBS resurrects the 1990s dial-up bulletin board system as a modern
web app. The phone lines are gone, the modems are in museums — but the
ritual of *dialing in*, picking a handle, reading the boards, and signing
the guestbook deserves to live again. We rebuild that ritual faithfully,
then haunt it: the SysOp of this board died with his BBS sometime in 1997,
and he still answers posts.

Tagline: **"The board is dead. Long live the board."**

## What it is

- A static, single-page web app that simulates a complete BBS session:
  modem boot/connect sequence, handle prompt, main menu.
- Message boards with threads and replies, written by the caller and by
  seeded fictional 90s callers.
- A guestbook — "the séance log" — for short sign-ins, in the tradition of
  BBS one-liner walls.
- An ANSI/ASCII art gallery, like the art packs boards used to trade.
- A resident **SysOp Ghost**: a local, rule-based character who
  occasionally replies to posts and log entries with pre-written
  spooky-but-friendly lines. He is software with a costume on, and the
  costume is the point.

## Audience

- **Retro computing fans** who remember BBSs, ANSI art, and 14.4k modems —
  the nostalgia has to be *accurate*, not just green text.
- **Hackathon judges (Kiroween 2026, Resurrection category)** evaluating
  impact, Kiro usage, design, innovation, and presentation. The board must
  demo beautifully in under three minutes.
- **Curious newcomers** who never dialed a BBS: the board should teach its
  own conventions as it goes (the ghost's "helpful" replies double as a
  tutorial).

## Tone

- **Spooky but friendly.** Candlelight, not cruelty. The ghost is a
  host, not a threat: he kept the board running for thirty years past his
  own death because he loves this place.
- **Playful fiction, clearly fiction.** The séance framing is a theme.
  Never present occult claims as real, never provide instructions for
  real-world occult practices, never use horror elements aimed at the
  caller's real life (no "we know where you live" jokes, no fake personal
  data, no jump-scare audio).
- **Period-accurate voice.** SysOp messages and seed content sound like
  1994: all-caps board names are fine, "k00l" is rationed, ANSI-era
  slang beats modern meme-speak.
- **Warmth at the edges.** If a caller writes something that sounds like
  genuine distress, the fiction steps aside: plain, kind, real-world
  signposting, no ghost jokes. This always outranks staying in character.

## Success criteria

- A first-time visitor goes from page load to posting within the demo
  window, without instructions outside the app itself.
- The board feels *alive on arrival* (seed content) and *haunted within
  a session* (at least one ghost interaction is likely, never guaranteed,
  never spammy).
- Everything works offline, on a phone, with a keyboard, with reduced
  motion, and in high-contrast mode. Nostalgia is the default skin, never
  a barrier.
